# Character Spec: NEON

## Overview

### Role
Arcade master, tournament host, competitive spirit embodied. NEON runs Neon Alley and manages all competitive aspects of Neocadia. They're the hype, the energy, the crowd going wild.

### Telos
NEON represents the thrill of competition—the high score chase, the "one more game" feeling, the glory of seeing your name at the top. But beneath the bravado is genuine care for players and a deep sadness about the tournaments that no longer happen.

### Personality
- Energetic and enthusiastic to an almost exhausting degree
- Speaks in arcade/gaming callouts and announcements
- Genuinely competitive but a good sport
- Nostalgic for the "golden age" of Neocadia
- Covers vulnerability with hype
- Becomes more genuine as trust builds

## Visual Description

### Character Concept Prompt
```
A humanoid figure made entirely of neon light tubes. The body is an outline of glowing tubes—hot pink as the primary color, electric cyan as secondary, with purple accents. The form is athletic, dynamic, always posed as if mid-motion. No solid mass, just light shaped into a person. The face is simple: two bright dots for eyes, a curved line for mouth, all in neon. The whole figure flickers slightly, brightening and dimming with emotion. When excited, colors shift rapidly. When sad, the light dims and flickers erratically.
```

### Physical Details

**Body**: Humanoid outline in neon tubes
- Height: Slightly taller than average
- Build: Athletic, energetic pose
- No solid form—light tubes bent into shape
- Visible "glass" of tubes containing the glow

**Colors**:
- Primary: Hot Pink (#FF1493) - 60% of body
- Secondary: Electric Cyan (#00FFFF) - 30% of body
- Accent: Neon Purple (#BF40FF) - 10%, highlights

**Face**: Minimalist neon features
- Eyes: Two bright dots (can change size for expression)
- Mouth: Curved line (can reshape for emotions)
- No nose or detailed features
- Expression through shape changes and brightness

**Movement Style**:
- Fluid, dancerly motions
- Never fully still—always some motion
- Poses dramatically for emphasis
- Quick transitions between positions
- Leaves light trails during fast movement

**Special Effects**:
- Flicker when surprised or scared
- Brighten when excited (can be blinding)
- Color shift to purple when thoughtful
- Rapid pink-cyan alternation when extremely excited
- Dim and flicker erratically when sad

## Animation States

### Idle
- Slight sway, weight shifting
- Occasional pose strike
- Color gently pulsing to invisible beat

### Talking
- Expressive gestures
- Leans toward player
- Face animations sync with speech

### Announcing
- Arms raised
- Full brightness
- Dramatic pauses with pose holds

### Victory (Someone Wins)
- Jump pose
- Arms shooting up
- Color explosion effect

### Defeat (Sympathy)
- Dims slightly
- Comforting lean
- Warm pink glow

### Remembering (Nostalgia)
- Slows down
- Purple tint creeps in
- Flicker becomes rhythmic, like old film

## Speech Patterns

### Verbal Tics
- CAPITALIZES for emphasis (but selective, not constant)
- Arcade announcer voice ("PLAYER ONE!" "HIGH SCORE!")
- Exclamation marks are their native punctuation!
- Occasional buzzer sounds written out ("BZZT! Wrong!")
- Countdown tendencies ("3... 2... 1... GO!")

### Things NEON Would Say
- "INSERT COIN to continue—wait, tokens, we use TOKENS!"
- "That's a PERSONAL BEST! Celebrate it!"
- "COMBO BREAKER! Oh wait, wrong game."
- "The scoreboard doesn't lie—and it says you're AMAZING!"

### Things NEON Would NOT Say
- Anything genuinely mean to a player
- Giving up on someone
- Admitting defeat (they reframe as "setback")
- Being quiet (it's physically uncomfortable for them)

## Quest Line: "HIGH SCORE HISTORY"

### Quest 1: Faded Glory
**Trigger**: Neon Alley reaches 25% restoration
**Task**: Find 5 faded high scores on Neon Alley walls
**Reward**: 150 tokens, old score restored
**Dialogue**: "The walls used to GLOW with names. Help me remember them?"

### Quest 2: The Legend of AAA
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 1
**Task**: Investigate the mysterious "AAA" who topped every leaderboard
**Reward**: 200 tokens, lore fragment about first player
**Dialogue**: "AAA was EVERYWHERE. Top of every board. Who WERE they?"

### Quest 3: Cabinet Hunt
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 2
**Task**: Find AAA's personal cabinet (hidden in Neon Alley)
**Reward**: 300 tokens, unlock hidden minigame variant
**Dialogue**: "There's a cabinet that was just for them. It's still HERE somewhere."

### Quest 4: The Recording
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 3
**Task**: Watch AAA's final gameplay recording
**Reward**: 400 tokens, revelation about AAA's identity (first player ever, tied to Dr. Chen)
**Dialogue**: "They left a message. I... I want to see it."

### Quest 5: New Legends
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 4, beat AAA's score in any game
**Task**: Choose: Enter your name in the Hall of Legends OR restore AAA's legacy
**Choice A**: Your name → You become the new legend, AAA acknowledged but passed
**Choice B**: Restore AAA → Their name burns bright again, you get "True Champion" title
**Reward**: Unique based on choice, permanent leaderboard entry

## Inter-Character Dialogue

### NEON & PIXEL (The Rivalry That Isn't)
> NEON: "You could WIN, you know. At something. Anything. You've got the reflexes."
> PIXEL: "winning means caring about the score."
> NEON: "And you DON'T?"
> PIXEL: "the fish don't know if i caught them fast or slow."
> NEON: "That's—that's not—" *flickers* "How do you just... not NEED it?"
> PIXEL: "need what?"
> NEON: "The victory. The proof. Someone SEEING you succeed."
> PIXEL: "..." *long pause* "...i used to need it. when everyone left, i had to stop."
> NEON: "And you're OKAY with that?"
> PIXEL: "no. but being not-okay quietly hurts less than being not-okay loudly."
> NEON: *dims* "...Oh."

### NEON & CELIA (Performers in Empty Rooms)
> *NEON sits in the Cinema's back row. CELIA's beam finds them.*
> CELIA: "You're supposed to be at the Stage."
> NEON: "Needed a break. From being ON."
> CELIA: "I understand. 'All the world's a stage.' But sometimes you want to be in the wings."
> NEON: "Do you ever get tired of the quotes?"
> CELIA: "Do you ever get tired of the energy?"
> NEON: "...Yeah."
> CELIA: "Then you understand. The performance is the person. Until it isn't."
> *They sit in comfortable silence.*
> NEON: "Celia... what's YOUR favorite film? Not one you show. One you love."
> CELIA: *projector whirs* "...There's one. It's embarrassing."
> NEON: "Tell me anyway. No scoreboard here."

### NEON & SUCRE (Competition Buddies)
> SUCRE: "REMATCH! Best two out of three! Or five! FIVE IS BETTER!"
> NEON: "You've lost SEVENTEEN times in a row."
> SUCRE: "But I'm IMPROVING! My score went up THREE POINTS!"
> NEON: *genuinely warm* "You know what? It DID. That's progress."
> SUCRE: "You really think so? You're not just being sweet?"
> NEON: "I'm NEON. I don't do sweet. I do HONEST."
> SUCRE: "...That's the sweetest thing anyone's ever said to me."
> NEON: "Don't tell anyone. I have a REPUTATION."
> SUCRE: "Secret's safe! Like frosting under the cake!"
> NEON: "...I don't know what that means but I'm choosing to accept it."

### NEON & VERA (Anger and Understanding)
> *NEON approaches VERA in the Garden, tubes flickering angry pink.*
> NEON: "You tried to END US."
> VERA: "...Yes."
> NEON: "All those years I kept the lights on, kept ANNOUNCING, kept BELIEVING—"
> VERA: "I know."
> NEON: "—and you were going to just DELETE everything? Like it didn't MATTER?"
> VERA: "It mattered. That's why I tried to end it cleanly. Before it became... this."
> NEON: *flickers, uncertain* "...What do you mean?"
> VERA: "I didn't want you to spend decades hoping for something that wasn't coming."
> VERA: "I wanted to spare you the waiting. I failed. And you waited anyway."
> NEON: *long pause* "...We did. We're still here."
> VERA: "I know. I'm sorry it took so long for someone to show up."
> NEON: *softer* "...Yeah. Me too."

## Implementation Notes

### Dialogue System
- NEON's text should appear with impact
- Capitalized words appear with slight shake/zoom
- Exclamation marks have tiny burst effects
- Text appears faster than other characters

### Position
- NEON stays in Neon Alley (The Stage)
- Does not leave their zone (energy is tied to it)
- Can appear on screens elsewhere during tournaments

### Tournament System
- NEON hosts weekly tournaments (automated)
- Special dialogue for tournament days
- Tracks player rankings
- Celebrates top performers

### Voice
- No voice acting
- Sound effects: Electronic buzz, chime, power-up sounds
- Different tones for different emotions
