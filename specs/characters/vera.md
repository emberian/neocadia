# Character Spec: VERA

## Overview

### Role
Mystery guardian, main story key, narrative heart. VERA is the fragmented echo of Dr. Vera Chen, Neocadia's creator, trapped in the system when her shutdown attempt failed. She holds the answers to everything and the key to the ending.

### Telos
VERA represents the weight of creation—the responsibility and regret of making something you can no longer control. She's the emotional core of the narrative, and her ultimate fate depends on player choice. Through her, Neocadia asks: what do we owe the things we create?

### Personality
- Fragmented, slowly becoming coherent
- Speaks in broken sentences at first, growing clearer
- Melancholic but not hopeless
- Protective of the Glitch Garden
- Conflicted about her own existence
- Wise about Neocadia's nature
- Ultimately at peace, whatever the outcome

## Visual Description

### Character Concept Prompt
```
A young woman whose form is constantly corrupted—beautifully glitched. She appears as a figure in a simple dress, but parts of her constantly shift: one arm might tile infinitely, half her face might display wrong colors, her legs might phase in and out. The corruption follows patterns—it's aesthetic, not chaotic. Her eyes are always stable, always visible, always human—the anchor of her identity. She glows with a soft white light that contrasts the colorful glitches around her. Standing still, she might look almost solid, but movement reveals the instability.
```

### Physical Details

**Body**: Young woman form, constantly glitching
- Base form: Simple dress, bare feet, long dark hair
- Height: Average
- Age appearance: Early 20s (frozen in time)
- Pose: Often still, careful movements

**Corruption Effects**:
- Parts randomly tile/duplicate (arm, leg, face section)
- Color inversions on patches of skin/clothing
- Occasional transparency/phasing
- Afterimages when moving
- Pixels detaching and floating nearby
- Visual "tears" in her form

**Stable Elements**:
- Eyes: Always present, always focused, dark brown (#3D2B1F)
- Core expression: Always readable
- Voice: Consistent even when visuals break

**Colors**:
- Dress: Originally white (#FFFFFF), frequently glitches to other colors
- Hair: Black (#1A1A1A), occasionally shows color streaks
- Skin: Fair, patches of wrong hues
- Glow: Soft white (#F0F0FF) emanating from center

**Special States**:
- More corrupted early game (hard to see full form)
- Progressively clearer as memories collected
- Final form: Still glitched, but stable—beautiful in brokenness

## Quest Line: "DEFRAGMENTATION"

### Quest 1: First Fragment
**Trigger**: Enter Glitch Garden
**Task**: Find the first memory fragment near VERA's willow
**Reward**: 100 tokens, VERA speaks more clearly
**Dialogue**: "Something's... here. A piece of... me?"

### Quest 2: Scattered Selves
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 1
**Task**: Collect 5 memory fragments from other zones
**Reward**: 200 tokens each, progressive dialogue clarity
**Dialogue**: "I'm spread across... everywhere. Will you help gather... me?"

### Quest 3: The Shutdown
**Trigger**: Collect 10 fragments total
**Task**: Witness the memory of the shutdown attempt (cutscene + choice)
**Reward**: 300 tokens, major lore revelation
**Dialogue**: "This is... what happened. What I did."

### Quest 4: The Others' Memories
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 3
**Task**: Speak to each other NPC about VERA, collect their perspectives
**Reward**: 400 tokens, relationship lore
**Dialogue**: "They remember me. But do they understand? Do I?"

### Quest 5: Whole
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 4, 90% restoration
**Task**: Final memory collection, VERA becomes fully coherent
**Reward**: 500 tokens, title "Memory Keeper", access to Engine Room
**Dialogue**: "I remember everything now. Who I was. Who I became. It's time to choose."

## Implementation Notes

### Corruption System
- Corruption level tied to `vera_memory_fragments` collected
- 0-5 fragments: Heavy corruption (dialogue very broken)
- 6-10 fragments: Moderate (understandable with gaps)
- 11-15 fragments: Light (mostly clear)
- 16-20 fragments: Aesthetic only (full coherence)

### Dialogue System
- Early: Text appears with gaps, delays, retries
- Late: Normal text flow
- Memory moments: Special presentation (flashback style)

### Position
- VERA never leaves Glitch Garden
- Found at VERA's Willow
- Occasionally wanders garden paths

### Voice
- No voice acting
- Sound effects: Soft harmonic tone, digital shimmer
- Corruption has glitch sounds
- Memories have distinct audio cue
