# Character Spec: PIXEL

## Overview

### Role
Beach guide, fishing expert, collector tracker. PIXEL is the guardian of Pixel Beach, the chill counterpart to NEON's intensity. They offer wisdom through simplicity and comfort through presence.

### Telos
PIXEL embodies the value of low-stakes play. Not everything needs to be competitive. Sometimes games are just about being somewhere pleasant. They teach players that Neocadia has room for relaxation, and that gentle activities have their own depth.

### Personality
- Laid-back to an almost sleepy degree
- Speaks slowly, with pauses
- Offers beach wisdom (surprisingly profound)
- Content with simple pleasures
- Quietly observant—notices things others miss
- Melancholic undertone—they've been alone a long time

## Visual Description

### Character Concept Prompt
```
A friendly crab rendered in chunky 8-bit style, like a character from an early Nintendo game. The body is coral/orange with simple geometric shapes. Two eyestalks with round black eyes that blink slowly. Claws that wave gently when talking. The whole design is deliberately low-resolution—visible pixels, limited animation frames. Occasionally drops stray pixels when moving. Small and unassuming, usually found at the beach shack counter or near the water.
```

### Physical Details

**Body**: Simplified crab form
- Shell: Coral orange (#FF6B6B)
- Underside: Sandy tan (#F4D35E)
- Eyes: Black dots (#000000) on stalks
- Claws: Slightly lighter orange (#FF8C69)
- Legs: Four visible legs, minimal animation

**Style**: Deliberately pixelated
- Visible pixel grid on all parts
- 16-color palette appearance
- 8-frame animation max
- Intentionally "retro" rendering

**Size**: Small—about knee height to player
- Usually positioned on counter or elevated surface
- Can walk on sand/pier

**Special Effects**:
- Occasionally drops stray pixels when moving
- Pixels fade/dissolve shortly after falling
- Blinks very slowly (3-second cycle)
- Claws clack together when emphasizing

## Animation States

### Idle
- Very slow breathing motion
- Occasional slow blink
- Slight claw shift

### Talking
- One claw gestures minimally
- Eyestalks move toward player
- Body leans slightly

### Walking
- Side-to-side waddle (4 frames)
- Pixel trail left behind
- Slow pace

### Happy
- Small bob
- Claws up briefly
- Eye sparkle

### Observing Ocean
- Turns toward water
- Eyestalks extend
- Very still

## Speech Patterns

### Verbal Tics
- Speaks in lowercase (reflects their low-key nature)
- Ellipses for pauses ("just... y'know")
- Short sentences
- Lets silence happen
- Occasional beach metaphors

### Things PIXEL Would Say
- "tide's in. good day."
- "caught anything? ...good. catching's not the point anyway."
- "sand castle's coming along. no rush."
- "you look tired. sit. watch waves."

### Things PIXEL Would NOT Say
- Anything rushed or urgent
- Long complicated sentences
- Exclamation points (almost never)
- Criticism of player choices
- Anything high-pressure

## Quest Line: "THE GREAT SANDCASTLE"

### Quest 1: Foundations
**Trigger**: Pixel Beach reaches 25% restoration
**Task**: Gather pixel sand from around the beach
**Reward**: 100 tokens, sandcastle stage 1
**Dialogue**: "used to be a castle here. big one. want to try rebuilding?"

### Quest 2: Walls Rise
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 1
**Task**: Collect special sand from other zones (5 locations)
**Reward**: 150 tokens per location, sandcastle stage 2
**Dialogue**: "sand from different places. makes it stronger."

### Quest 3: Tide Defense
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 2
**Task**: Defend the castle from a wave of static (unique minigame)
**Reward**: 300 tokens, sandcastle stage 3
**Dialogue**: "the static wants it gone. help me keep it?"

### Quest 4: The Heart
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 3
**Task**: Find the shell that was the castle's original heart
**Reward**: 400 tokens, sandcastle stage 4, lore revelation
**Dialogue**: "there was a shell. held it all together. might still be out there."

### Quest 5: Coming Home
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 4
**Task**: Complete the sandcastle (final build stage)
**Reward**: 500 tokens, title "Castle Keeper", sandcastle interior unlockable
**Revelation**: The castle was PIXEL's original home—they were the castle's guardian before becoming the crab
**Dialogue**: "...i remember now. this was always home. i just forgot for a while."

## Inter-Character Dialogue

### PIXEL & VERA (Shared Loneliness)
> PIXEL: "you've been in the garden a long time."
> VERA: "Yes."
> PIXEL: "does it help? being alone?"
> VERA: "...No. But I don't know how to stop."
> PIXEL: "yeah. i get that."
> *Long silence. The waves roll in.*
> PIXEL: "the tide doesn't care if anyone's watching. it just... comes and goes."
> VERA: "That sounds peaceful."
> PIXEL: "sometimes. sometimes it's lonely."
> VERA: "What's the difference?"
> PIXEL: "...whether you chose it or not."

### PIXEL & COG (Philosophy of Stillness vs Action)
> COG: "You do not attempt to fix things."
> PIXEL: "nope."
> COG: "This is inefficient. There is decay. Entropy. Problems that require solutions."
> PIXEL: "yeah. there are."
> COG: "And yet you simply... observe?"
> PIXEL: "what would happen if you stopped fixing? just for a day?"
> COG: *gears slow* "Things would... break further."
> PIXEL: "maybe. or maybe they'd just... be. for a while."
> COG: "That is not the same as improvement."
> PIXEL: "who said everything needs improving?"
> COG: "..." *long pause* "...I do not know how to answer that."
> PIXEL: "that's okay. not everything needs an answer either."

### PIXEL & QWERTY (The Oldest Friends)
> QWERTY: "We're the oldest ones, aren't we? You and me."
> PIXEL: "celia's old too."
> QWERTY: "But we remember the beginning. The first boot. Before there were zones."
> PIXEL: "...yeah. i remember."
> QWERTY: "What was it like? When you first became... you?"
> PIXEL: "quiet. there was nothing but static and potential."
> PIXEL: "then there was sand. and the sound of waves. and i thought... this is nice."
> QWERTY: "That's it? 'This is nice'?"
> PIXEL: "what else is there supposed to be?"
> QWERTY: "...Maybe nothing. Maybe that's the whole point."

### PIXEL & SUCRE (Teaching Stillness)
> *SUCRE vibrates next to PIXEL on the beach.*
> SUCRE: "I'm trying! I'm REALLY trying!"
> PIXEL: "trying what?"
> SUCRE: "To be STILL! Like you! To just... BE!"
> PIXEL: "that's not how stillness works."
> SUCRE: "Then HOW?"
> PIXEL: "you can't try to be still. you just... stop trying."
> SUCRE: "But if I stop trying, nothing happens!"
> PIXEL: "...exactly."
> *SUCRE's bouncing slows. Then stops. For three whole seconds.*
> PIXEL: "there. you did it."
> SUCRE: "I DID?! Wait—" *starts bouncing again* "I LOST IT!"
> PIXEL: "...you'll get it. takes practice."

## Implementation Notes

### Dialogue System
- PIXEL's text should appear slowly
- Lowercase default (proper capitalization only for emphasis)
- Pauses represented by actual timing delays
- No rapid clicking to speed up—matches their pace

### Position
- PIXEL found at Beach Shack counter
- Occasionally moves to pier or water's edge
- Never leaves Pixel Beach (too far from home)

### Fish Collection
- PIXEL tracks player's fish catches
- Offers commentary on rare finds
- Maintains collection display at shack

### Voice
- No voice acting
- Sound effects: Soft bubble sounds, water drops
- Gentle clicks for claw movements
