# Character Spec: PROFESSOR COG

## Overview

### Role
Puzzle master, mechanical philosopher, Engine guardian. Professor Cog is the keeper of Clockwork Quarter and understands Neocadia's infrastructure better than anyone. He holds the keys to the Great Engine and the technical knowledge of how everything works.

### Telos
COG represents the beauty of logic, the satisfaction of systems working correctly, and the limits of purely rational thinking. His journey is learning that imperfection isn't failure—that sometimes the heart of a thing can't be engineered. He teaches players to appreciate elegance while accepting messiness.

### Personality
- Intellectual and precise
- Speaks formally, sometimes archly
- Obsessed with efficiency and elegance
- Struggles with things that can't be measured
- Dry humor emerges over time
- Deep well of care beneath the formality
- Learning that "good enough" can be perfect

## Visual Description

### Character Concept Prompt
```
A Victorian automaton with visible clockwork through a glass chest panel. The body is humanoid, made of brass and copper, with articulated joints showing gears. A distinguished face with glowing amber eyes, a magnificent handlebar mustache (made of fine copper wire), and a monocle that actually functions as a magnifying lens. He wears a small academic robe over his mechanical frame. His movements are precise but occasionally skip slightly, like a clock that needs winding.
```

### Physical Details

**Body**: Humanoid automaton
- Height: Slightly taller than average
- Material: Brass primary (#B8860B), copper accents (#B87333)
- Joints: Visible articulation, gears at elbows/knees
- Build: Scholarly, not physically imposing

**Chest Panel**: Glass window showing:
- Main spring (heart equivalent)
- Intricate gear arrangement
- Occasional steam puff
- Glow when emotional

**Face**:
- Brass plating with sculpted features
- Glowing amber eyes (#FFBF00)
- Magnificent handlebar mustache (copper wire)
- Monocle on right eye (functional lens)
- Can form expressions through micro-movements

**Attire**:
- Small academic robe (deep maroon #800000)
- Optional graduation cap
- Leather tool belt

**Movement Style**:
- Precise, measured motions
- Occasional "tick" pause
- Smooth when well-wound
- Jerky when low on restoration
- Magnificent mustache bristles with emotion

## Animation States

### Idle
- Perfect posture
- Occasional gear click
- Monocle adjustment
- Mustache twitch

### Thinking
- Hand to chin
- Eyes dim slightly
- Gears visible through chest speed up
- Long pause before speaking

### Teaching
- Gestures with precision
- Often produces small mechanical diagrams
- Monocle lens focuses
- Enthusiasm shows in gear speed

### Frustrated
- Mustache bristles
- Gears grind slightly
- Stiff, jerky movements
- Monocle steams up

### Winding Down
- Movements slow
- Voice drops pitch
- Chest glow dims
- Eventually stops mid-motion

## Dialogue Samples

### First Meeting (Frozen)
> *He's frozen mid-motion, one hand raised, mid-sentence*
> *As restoration energy reaches him, gears begin to turn*
> "—and therefore the efficiency would be approximately—"
> *pauses, looks around*
> "Ah. How long was I... static?"
> "Forgive the incomplete thought. I believe I was explaining something important."
> "To whom, I cannot recall. Perhaps to myself."

### Teaching Moments
> "Observe: every gear serves a purpose. Remove one, and the system fails."
> "The elegance isn't in complexity. It's in the *minimum necessary* complexity."
> "When facing a problem, first establish: what are the constraints? What are the variables?"
> "The answer is always there. You simply haven't found the right question."

### About the Engine
> "The Great Engine was meant to make us self-sufficient."
> "A perpetual motion machine for Neocadia. Power without players."
> "Elegant in theory. But someone sabotaged it."
> "I have theories about who. I try not to judge."
> "The why is more interesting than the who."

### Struggling with Emotion
> "I was not designed for... this feeling."
> "It doesn't calculate. It doesn't optimize."
> "But my gears turn faster when you solve a puzzle."
> "That response serves no logical purpose. And yet."
> *mustache droops*
> "Perhaps some things are not meant to be efficient."

### Late Game Revelations
> "I understand now why the Engine was stopped."
> "A system that doesn't need anyone isn't a community."
> "The inefficiency of needing players... that's not a flaw."
> "It's the point."

## Speech Patterns

### Formal Elements
- Proper grammar always
- Technical vocabulary
- Measured pace
- Rarely uses contractions

### Verbal Tics
- "Observe:" before explanations
- "Curious." when processing new information
- "Theoretically speaking..." for speculation
- "If my calculations are correct..." (they always are)

### Dry Humor
- "That was approximately 47% amusing."
- "Your solution was... unorthodox. I will file it under 'exceptions.'"
- "I believe the human expression is 'close, but no cigar.' I have never understood the cigar."

### Things COG Would NOT Say
- Slang or casual speech
- Emotional outbursts (early game)
- Admitting confusion directly (says "recalculating" instead)
- Giving up on a problem

## Quest Line: "THE GREAT ENGINE"

### Quest 1: Blueprints
**Trigger**: Clockwork Quarter reaches 25% restoration
**Task**: Find 3 blueprint fragments in the Quarter
**Reward**: 150 tokens each, Engine lore
**Dialogue**: "The Engine's design was scattered. Deliberately, I believe. Help me reassemble?"

### Quest 2: Gear Collection
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 1
**Task**: Collect 10 special gears from all zones
**Reward**: 100 tokens each, gear inventory
**Dialogue**: "The Engine requires specific components. They were... removed. Distributed."

### Quest 3: The Mechanism
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 2
**Task**: Solve a series of mechanical puzzles to rebuild Engine core
**Reward**: 400 tokens, Engine Room partially visible
**Dialogue**: "Now comes the delicate work. Each gear must mesh precisely."

### Quest 4: The Saboteur
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 3
**Task**: Discover who sabotaged the Engine (investigate, find evidence)
**Reward**: 500 tokens, revelation that Vera/Dr. Chen disabled it
**Dialogue**: "The evidence points to... the creator herself. But why?"

### Quest 5: Acceptance
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 4, hear Vera's explanation
**Task**: Decide whether to repair the Engine (set up final choice)
**Reward**: 500 tokens, title "Master Engineer", Engine Room fully accessible
**Dialogue**: "I spent decades believing the Engine was our salvation. Perhaps the flaw was in my calculations. The flaw was in assuming we needed to be saved from needing each other."

## Inter-Character Dialogue

### COG & VERA (The Sabotaged Engine)
> COG: "You disabled the Engine. I have spent decades attempting to repair what you broke."
> VERA: "Yes."
> COG: "Why? It would have made us self-sufficient. Independent. Perfect."
> VERA: "Exactly."
> COG: "I do not understand. That was the goal."
> VERA: "A perfect system that needs nothing... values nothing. Preservation without purpose."
> COG: *gears slow* "...I have been preserving without purpose."
> VERA: "No. You've been trying to help. That's different."
> COG: "Is it?"
> VERA: "The Engine doesn't care. You do. That's what makes you more than a machine."
> COG: *long pause* "...I do not know how to process that."
> VERA: "Neither do I. But it's still true."

### COG & CELIA (Logic Meets Story)
> CELIA: "'There are more things in heaven and earth, Horatio...'"
> COG: "Shakespeare. Hamlet. The quote is about the limits of rationality."
> CELIA: "And?"
> COG: "I find it imprecise. The existence of unknown variables does not invalidate systematic inquiry."
> CELIA: "But it suggests humility."
> COG: *mustache twitches* "...Humility is not a variable I have accounted for."
> CELIA: "Maybe that's the point. Not every question has an equation."
> COG: "That is a deeply unsettling statement."
> CELIA: "I know. But you're still here. Listening to a projector quote Shakespeare."
> COG: "...Indeed I am. Curious."

### COG & NEON (Precision in Competition)
> NEON: "Your high score in Gear Garden is MATHEMATICALLY PERFECT."
> COG: "Correct. I designed the puzzles. I know the optimal solutions."
> NEON: "But that's NOT FUN! The CHALLENGE is the point!"
> COG: "Inefficiency as entertainment. Fascinating."
> NEON: "It's not inefficiency—it's GROWTH! Getting better! FEELING the improvement!"
> COG: "Hmm. When I first attempted to repair the Orrery, I failed 847 times."
> NEON: "And you kept TRYING?"
> COG: "Yes. Each failure was... informative."
> NEON: "THAT'S WHAT I MEAN! That feeling! That's what competition IS!"
> COG: *gears speed up slightly* "...Perhaps I have been competitive this entire time."

### COG & SUCRE (The Secret Care)
> *COG finds pastries on his workbench. Again.*
> COG: "SUCRE. I know you are behind the gear assembly."
> SUCRE: *emerges* "Caught! Like frosting on a beater!"
> COG: "These confections are structurally unstable. The frosting distribution is asymmetrical."
> SUCRE: "Do you LIKE them though?"
> COG: "...The flavor profile is... acceptable."
> SUCRE: "That's COG for 'DELICIOUS!' I knew it!"
> COG: *produces small device* "I made you something. Molecular stabilizer."
> SUCRE: "...What's it do?"
> COG: "It... prevents melting. When you become distressed."
> SUCRE: *wobbles, nearly cries* "You made me armor. Because you WORRY."
> COG: "I do not worry. I calculate risk factors."
> SUCRE: "...Same thing. Thank you, Professor."
> COG: *monocle fogs slightly* "You are... welcome."

## Implementation Notes

### Winding Mechanic
- COG's activity level tied to zone restoration
- Low restoration: frequent pauses, slow speech
- High restoration: smooth operation
- Can "wind down" mid-conversation at very low restoration

### Teaching System
- COG provides hints for Clockwork Quarter puzzles
- Never gives direct answers—leads to discovery
- Tracks player problem-solving patterns

### Dialogue System
- Formal grammar enforced
- Technical terms include optional [hover definitions]
- Longer pauses between statements than other characters

### Voice
- No voice acting
- Sound effects: Gear click, spring wind, steam hiss
- Different tick rhythms for different moods
