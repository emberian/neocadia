# Zone Spec: Starlight Cinema

## Overview

### Telos
Starlight Cinema is about stories—their power, their persistence, their ability to outlast the tellers. It's the most emotionally resonant zone, where narrative itself is the magic. It asks: what happens to stories when no one watches them anymore? And answers: they wait, patiently, for someone to remember.

### Ludos
Games here are about narrative comprehension, cultural connection, and dramatic timing. Trivia rewards broad knowledge, scene recreation tests reflexes and memory, and the Director's Cut puzzle lets players create meaning from fragments. Perfect for players who love story over mechanics.

## Visual Description

### First Impression Prompt
```
A 1920s movie palace that's seen better days but still holds magic. Velvet curtains (faded crimson) frame a massive silver screen. Art deco gilt peels from the walls but catches light beautifully. Rows of empty seats stretch toward the screen, dust motes visible in the projector beam. Old film reels and posters from movies that never existed line the walls. A film projector with a face sits in the back, its light beam showing memories. The aesthetic is golden age Hollywood meets vaporwave.
```

### Detailed Environment

**Auditorium**: Main space with rows of velvet seats (faded crimson #8B0000 → restored #DC143C). Art deco patterns on walls and ceiling in gold (#FFD700) over deep purple (#2E0854). Chandeliers hang (dim → brilliant). Center aisle leads to the screen.

**The Screen**: Massive silver screen (actually silver-white #C0C0C0) that plays fragments of films, memories, abstract visuals. When idle, shows slowly shifting film grain. Active during games or story sequences.

**Projector Booth**: Elevated booth at rear where CELIA lives. Visible through windows—warm glow, mechanical whirring, occasionally a face in the light beam. Accessible as a clickable area.

**Lobby/Concession**: Smaller connected area with:
- Concession stand (popcorn machine, candy displays)
- Ticket booth (where daily rewards appear)
- Movie posters on walls
- Small velvet rope barriers

**Film Archives**: Side room accessible at higher restoration. Shelves of film reels, some glowing (collected), some dim (not yet found). Filing cabinets with lore documents.

**The Stage**: Area beneath the screen with faded footlights, small podium—used for special story events, dramatic moments, CELIA's pronouncements.

## Restoration States

### Stage 1: Flickering (0-25%)
- Auditorium: Dark, most seats obscured by shadows
- Screen: Static and fragments, rarely coherent
- CELIA: Dim projector, speaks only in broken quotes
- Posters: Faded to near-illegibility
- Lobby: Closed off, visible but inaccessible

### Stage 2: Awakening (25-50%)
- Auditorium: Visible, chandeliers provide ambient light
- Screen: Shows fragments, attract mode works
- CELIA: Present, starting to find her own voice
- Posters: Legible, colors muted
- Lobby: Accessible, concession stand half-stocked

### Stage 3: Thriving (50-85%)
- Auditorium: Comfortable, inviting
- Screen: Plays clips, games work fully
- CELIA: Full dialogue, dramatic personality
- Posters: Vibrant, some animated
- Film Archives: Unlocked, collectibles available

### Stage 4: Radiant (85-100%)
- Auditorium: Spectacular, premiere-night ambiance
- Screen: Full features playable, special content
- CELIA: At her best, directing special events
- Everything: Golden age glory restored
- Stage: Active with footlights, events possible

## Key Landmarks

### The Silver Screen
- **Function**: Game display, story sequences, visual centerpiece
- **Interaction**: Games played "on" the screen; cutscenes display here
- **Art Needs**: Large screen with film grain texture, multiple content overlays, curtain open/close animation

### Projector Booth (CELIA)
- **Function**: CELIA's home, dialogue hub, quest center
- **Interaction**: Click to talk to CELIA, receive quests, learn lore
- **Art Needs**: Booth interior, projector equipment, CELIA's position, light beam

### Velvet Seats
- **Function**: Atmosphere, potential interactive spots
- **Interaction**: Click a seat to "sit" (optional idle mode), some seats have hidden items
- **Art Needs**: Seat rows, individual clickable seats, sitting animation

### The Lobby
- **Function**: Secondary area, concession, ticket booth rewards
- **Interaction**: Navigate to separate scene; collect daily ticket
- **Art Needs**: Full lobby environment, concession stand, ticket booth, posters

### Film Archives
- **Function**: Collectible tracking, lore deep-dive
- **Interaction**: Browse collected reels, read lore documents, track completion
- **Art Needs**: Archive room, shelved reels (dim/glowing), filing cabinets

### The Stage
- **Function**: Dramatic moments, story events, CELIA speeches
- **Interaction**: Context-sensitive; activates during story beats
- **Art Needs**: Stage area, footlights, podium, curtain interaction

## Ambient Details

### Background Animations
- Projector beam with visible dust motes
- Film grain overlay on screen (subtle, continuous)
- Curtains gently moving (as if from drafts)
- Chandelier crystals occasionally catching light
- Shadows of "audience" in peripheral vision (high restoration)

### Background Characters
- At high restoration: silhouettes in seats, watching
- CELIA's beam occasionally shows faces, memories
- Phantom ushers walking aisles (very subtle)

### Environmental Sounds
- Baseline: Soft orchestral ambient, jazzy undertones
- Projector: Mechanical whirr (continuous)
- Film: Subtle crackle/pop of old reels
- Audience: Occasional cough, rustle (high restoration)
- CELIA: Her beam has a warm hum

## Sound Design

### Music
- **Style**: Jazz standards, orchestral swells, film score pastiche
- **Base Track**: "Silver Screen Dreams" - nostalgic, slightly melancholy, hopeful
- **Dynamic Layers**:
  - Layer 1 (0-25%): Solo piano, distant, echoing
  - Layer 2 (25-50%): Add bass, light drums, warmth
  - Layer 3 (50-85%): Full jazz combo, occasional strings
  - Layer 4 (85-100%): Full orchestra moments, triumphant swells

### Sound Effects Needed
- Projector mechanical hum/click
- Film reel advance
- Curtain rustle
- Seat fold up/down
- Popcorn ambient (lobby)
- Audience murmur
- Spotlight activation
- CELIA's beam hum
- Film grain crackle
- Dramatic sting (for revelations)

```
Lobby → Starlight Cinema (auditorium)
        ├─→ Projector Booth (CELIA)
        ├─→ The Screen → Scene Stealer
        ├─→ Trivia Seat → Reel Trivia
        ├─→ Director's Chair → Director's Cut
        ├─→ To Lobby → Cinema Lobby
        │   ├─→ Concession (cosmetic)
        │   ├─→ Ticket Booth (daily)
        │   └─→ Back to Auditorium
        ├─→ Film Archives (when unlocked)
        ├─→ The Stage (contextual)
        └─→ Exit → Lobby (main)
```

interface StarlightCinemaState {
  restoration_percent: number;

### Screen Content System
- Screen should support multiple content types:
  1. Idle: film grain with occasional fragments
  2. Attract: preview clips for games
  3. Game: full game render
  4. Story: cutscene playback
  5. Credits: end-game sequence
- Content managed by CELIA (narratively and technically)
