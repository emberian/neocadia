# Zone Spec: Clockwork Quarter

## Overview

### Telos
Clockwork Quarter represents the mind—logic, precision, the satisfaction of things working exactly as designed. It's for players who love systems, who find joy in optimization, who appreciate the beauty of well-oiled machinery. It also carries the weight of Neocadia's infrastructure: the Great Engine that could save everything.

### Ludos
Games here are puzzle-focused: spatial reasoning, timing, strategy. Gear Garden tests mechanical intuition, Tick Defense challenges tactical planning, and Spring Loaded rewards physics understanding. Satisfying to solve, deep to master.

## Visual Description

### First Impression Prompt
```
A steampunk quarter where Victorian engineering meets circuit boards. Brass gears of all sizes turn on walls and floors. Pipes carry steam that vents in rhythmic puffs. The architecture is wrought iron and polished copper, with glass panels revealing clockwork innards. Mechanical birds perch on ledges. A massive orrery rotates overhead. Professor Cog's workshop dominates one corner, cluttered with blueprints and prototypes. Everything ticks, clicks, and whirs in satisfying synchronization.
```

### Detailed Environment

**Floors and Surfaces**: Brass grating (#B8860B) over visible clockwork underneath. Some sections are glass, showing gears and pistons below. Copper accent strips (#B87333). Oil stains on well-worn paths.

**Walls**: Combination of:
- Exposed brick (#8B4513) with brass fixtures
- Glass panels showing internal mechanisms
- Wrought iron frameworks
- Mounted gears (some decorative, some functional)
- Blueprint papers pinned up

**Machinery**: Everywhere. Gears of sizes from thumbnail to person-height. Pistons pumping. Steam vents releasing on schedule. Pressure gauges. Conveyor belts (some carry small items). Everything interconnected.

**The Orrery**: Massive overhead mechanical solar system. Brass planets on arms rotating around a central sun (warm lamp). At full restoration, it's a spectacular kinetic sculpture.

**Professor Cog's Workshop**: Open-air workshop space filled with:
- Workbenches covered in parts
- Blueprints pinned to boards
- Prototype machines (some working, some not)
- Professor Cog's perch (a raised chair)
- Tools hanging on walls

**The Great Engine Room**: Locked door until late game. When accessible, reveals the massive, dormant Engine at Neocadia's heart. The zone's ultimate restoration goal.

**Steam Vents**: Periodic steam releases that are both atmospheric and potential puzzle elements in games.

## Restoration States

### Stage 1: Flickering (0-25%)
- Machinery: Mostly frozen, occasional stutter
- Orrery: Stuck, planets misaligned
- Steam: Irregular, some vents broken
- Cog: Frozen mid-motion, not yet wound
- Engine Room: Locked, no indication

### Stage 2: Awakening (25-50%)
- Machinery: Running but rough, some grinding
- Orrery: Slow rotation, jerky
- Steam: Regular but weak
- Cog: Wound up, speaking, enthusiastic
- Engine Room: Door visible, locked

### Stage 3: Thriving (50-85%)
- Machinery: Smooth, synchronized, beautiful
- Orrery: Full motion, planets gleaming
- Steam: Rhythmic, powerful, satisfying
- Cog: In his element, teaching, questing
- Engine Room: Can peer through keyhole

### Stage 4: Radiant (85-100%)
- Machinery: Perfection in motion, concert of clicks
- Orrery: Spectacular, with light effects
- Steam: Part of the music, timed puffs
- Cog: Full potential, reveals Engine secrets
- Engine Room: Unlockable for final act

## Key Landmarks

### Professor Cog's Workshop
- **Function**: Cog's home, quest hub, learning center
- **Interaction**: Click to talk to Cog, receive blueprints, learn mechanics
- **Art Needs**: Workshop environment, workbenches, blueprints, tools, Cog's chair

### The Orrery
- **Function**: Visual centerpiece, time indicator
- **Interaction**: View animation; at full restoration, clicking reveals hidden lore
- **Art Needs**: Brass solar system, rotating arms, planets, central sun lamp

### Gear Garden Station
- **Function**: Gear Garden game access
- **Interaction**: Click puzzle board to play
- **Art Needs**: Wall-mounted puzzle board with gear slots, indicator lights

### Defense Platform
- **Function**: Tick Defense game access
- **Interaction**: Click platform to start tower defense
- **Art Needs**: Elevated platform with miniature conveyor/path, tower slots

### Spring Chamber
- **Function**: Spring Loaded game access
- **Interaction**: Click launcher apparatus to play physics puzzles
- **Art Needs**: Mechanical launcher, target indicators, spring mechanisms

### The Great Engine Room Door
- **Function**: Late-game story access
- **Interaction**: Locked until story progression; then enter for Engine content
- **Art Needs**: Massive brass door with intricate lock, keyhole, eventual interior

## Ambient Details

### Background Animations
- Gears turning (multiple speeds, synchronized)
- Pistons pumping (rhythmic)

- Baseline: Tick-tock ambient, baroque undertones
- Gears: Constant clicking, grinding (varies with restoration)
- Steam: Periodic hiss and release
- Pistons: Rhythmic pump
- Orrery: Deep rotation sound
- Cog: Mechanical speech sounds, whirring thought

## Sound Design

### Music
- **Style**: Mechanical rhythms, baroque influences, clockwork soundtrack
- **Base Track**: "The Ticking Heart" - precise, layered, building
- **Dynamic Layers**:
  - Layer 1 (0-25%): Simple tick, occasional tone
  - Layer 2 (25-50%): Add bass rhythm, harpsichord hints
  - Layer 3 (50-85%): Full baroque-mechanical fusion
  - Layer 4 (85-100%): Grand, triumphant, Engine theme

### Sound Effects Needed
- Gear click/turn (multiple sizes)
- Piston pump
- Steam hiss/release
- Spring wind/release
- Brass footstep
- Mechanical bird chirp
- Blueprint rustle
- Cog's voice (mechanical, thoughtful)
- Engine Room door (heavy, brass)
- Success chime (satisfying click)
- Failure sound (gear grind)

## Implementation Notes

### Navigation Flow
```
Lobby → Clockwork Quarter (main area)
        ├─→ Cog's Workshop (Professor Cog)
        ├─→ The Orrery (view, late interaction)
        ├─→ Gear Garden Station → Gear Garden
        ├─→ Defense Platform → Tick Defense
        ├─→ Spring Chamber → Spring Loaded
        ├─→ Engine Room Door → Engine Room (late game)
        └─→ Exit → Lobby
```

### Data Required
```typescript
interface ClockworkQuarterState {
  restoration_percent: number;
  cog_quest_stage: number;
  blueprints_collected: BlueprintId[];
  gears_collected: number; // for Engine
  engine_room_unlocked: boolean;
  engine_activated: boolean; // endgame
  gear_garden_progress: PuzzleProgress;
  tick_defense_high_wave: number;
}
```

### Synchronization System
- All mechanical animations should be synchronized to a master clock
- This creates the "everything working together" feeling
- Master tick rate: 120 BPM (0.5 second per beat)
- Different elements tick on different beat divisions
- Music should sync to this same clock
