# Color Palettes

## Overview

Each zone has a distinct palette that creates its mood while harmonizing with the overall Neocadia aesthetic. Colors should be vibrant but not garish, warm but not washed out.

---

## Palette Harmony

### Cross-Zone Consistency
- Token Gold (#FFD700) same everywhere
- Static Gray (#808080) same everywhere
- Portal doors hint at destination palette
- Transitions blend smoothly

### Accessibility
All palettes tested for:
- Colorblind visibility
- Sufficient contrast
- Readable text overlays

### Dark Mode Variants
Not currently planned, but palettes chosen to work in dim viewing conditions.
