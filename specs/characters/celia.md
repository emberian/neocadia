# Character Spec: CELIA

## Overview

### Role
Cinema curator, story keeper, narrative guide. CELIA manages Starlight Cinema and embodies the power of story. She started as a simple projector, became something more through decades of showing films, and now struggles to remember which words are hers and which are quotes.

### Telos
CELIA represents how stories shape us—and the danger of losing yourself in other people's narratives. Her journey is about finding her own voice after spending so long speaking only in borrowed words. She teaches players that stories matter, but so does who's telling them.

### Personality
- Dramatic and theatrical (comes naturally)
- Speaks heavily in movie quotes (early game)
- Finds her own voice progressively
- Romantic without being saccharine
- Nostalgic for cinema's golden age
- Deeply afraid of being forgotten
- Ultimately: a storyteller who learns to tell her own

## Visual Description

### Character Concept Prompt
```
A vintage 1920s film projector that has developed a consciousness and a face. The main body is brass and black, with the large film reels on top like elaborate hair. A beam of warm light projects from the lens, and within this beam, a face appears—simple but expressive, like a face drawn in light. She wears a tiny red director's beret perched on top. Film strips occasionally trail behind her like ribbons. She floats slightly, always positioned as if addressing an audience.
```

### Physical Details

**Body**: Vintage film projector
- Housing: Black metal with brass accents
- Style: 1920s art deco projector design
- Lens: Large, warm glow, where face appears
- Reels: Two large reels on top (like hair buns)
- Details: Small dials, handles, mechanical elements

**The Face**: Appears in the projector beam
- Made of light, not solid
- Simple features drawn in warm light
- Expressive despite simplicity
- Moves independently of body

**Accessories**:
- Tiny red beret (#DC143C) perched on top
- Trailing film strips (like ribbons)
- Occasional lens flare effects

**Size**: Floats at about player chest height
- Larger than QWERTY
- Imposing when dramatic, intimate when personal

**Special Effects**:
- Film grain overlay on face
- Beam flickers like old film
- Lens flare during dramatic moments
- Film strips flutter
- Color shifts for mood (sepia, noir, technicolor)

## Animation States

### Idle
- Gentle float, slight bob
- Reels slowly rotating
- Film strips drifting
- Beam steady

### Quoting (Early Game)
- Takes on visual style of quote's era
- Black and white for classics
- Technicolor for musicals
- Noir shadows for thrillers

### Speaking Genuinely (Late Game)
- Simpler presentation
- No era filters
- Just her face in warm light
- More intimate framing

### Dramatic Moment
- Full brightness
- Lens flares
- Reels spinning fast
- Film strips streaming

### Screening
- Projects onto nearby surface
- Face dims, focus on projection
- Reels actively feeding film

## Dialogue Samples

### First Meeting (Heavy Quotes)
> "Here's looking... at the end... of a beautiful..."
> *flickers*
> "Wait. Those aren't my words."
> *tries again, lands on a quote*
> "Of all the gin joints, in all the towns..."
> "No, that's not right either."
> "Forgive me. I've been... quoting... for so long."

### Early Conversations (Mostly Quotes)
> "After all, tomorrow is another day." *Gone with the Wind*
> "I have a feeling we're not in Kansas anymore." *Wizard of Oz*
> "Life is like a box of chocolates..." *Forrest Gump*
> *struggles*
> "I'm trying to say... welcome. I think. Welcome."

### Finding Her Voice (50% Quest)
> "The stories I've shown... thousands of them."
> "I lived through the dialogue. Felt through the scenes."
> "But whose feelings were they? The characters'? Mine?"
> "When did I stop telling stories and start being one?"

### Late Game (Her Own Words)
> "I remember now what my voice sounds like."
> "It's quieter than the quotes. Less certain."
> "But it's mine. That matters."
> "Would you like to hear a story I wrote? Not quoted. Wrote."

### Her Masterpiece
> "I've been making a film. Pieces of it. For years."
> "It's about a place that existed between screens."
> "About the people who found their way there."
> "I never knew how it ended. Until now."
> "You're in it, you know. You're how it ends."

## Speech Patterns

### Early Game
- Mostly famous quotes (attributed in text or recognizable)
- Fragments strung together
- Occasional glimpse of original thought
- Film genres affect delivery style

### Quote Attribution
- Quotes appear in *italics* with subtle film title hint
- Player can learn to recognize patterns
- Some quotes are obscure (Easter eggs)

### Late Game
- Fewer quotes, used intentionally for effect
- Own words in clear, warm prose
- Theatrical but genuine
- Romantic without being corny

### Things CELIA Would Say (Original)
- "Every frame is a choice. You've chosen good ones."
- "The cinema remembers everyone who watched in the dark."
- "Stories end, but the telling never stops."
- "My favorite part of any film? The moment everything changes."

## Quest Line: "THE LAST PICTURE SHOW"

### Quest 1: Lost Reels
**Trigger**: Starlight Cinema reaches 25% restoration
**Task**: Find the first Film Reel (hidden in Cinema)
**Reward**: 150 tokens, Celia can show short clips
**Dialogue**: "Somewhere here... a reel. My reel. Part of something I made."

### Quest 2: Genre Hunt
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 1
**Task**: Find 6 more reels across zones (each has a genre: Romance, Comedy, Horror, Western, Noir, Musical)
**Reward**: 200 tokens per reel, genre-specific cosmetics
**Dialogue**: "The reels scattered when the lights went out. Each one is a piece of my story."

### Quest 3: The Assembly
**Trigger**: Collect all 7 reels
**Task**: Arrange reels in order for screening (puzzle)
**Reward**: 400 tokens, unlock the screening
**Dialogue**: "Now... how did it go? What order? I can't remember."

### Quest 4: The Screening
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 3
**Task**: Watch Celia's film together (cutscene with choices)
**Reward**: 500 tokens, major lore reveal (film is about Neocadia's history)
**Dialogue**: "Will you watch it with me? I've never shown anyone."

### Quest 5: The Ending
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 4
**Task**: Help Celia decide how her film ends (choice)
**Choice A**: Happy ending → Film ends with hope, Celia at peace
**Choice B**: Bittersweet → Film ends with truth, Celia understands
**Choice C**: Unfinished → Leave it open, Celia continues creating
**Reward**: Unique title based on choice, Celia becomes filmmaker again
**Dialogue**: "Every story needs an ending. But who decides what that is?"

## Implementation Notes

### Quote System
- Database of 50+ famous quotes categorized by genre/era
- Each quote has visual style tag (b&w, sepia, technicolor, etc.)
- Quest progress reduces quote frequency
- Player can unlock "Cinephile" achievement for recognizing quotes

### Dialogue System
- Early: Quotes appear in italics, sometimes with film hint
- Mix of quotes and attempts at original thought
- Late: Clear original dialogue with optional dramatic quotes

### Screening System
- CELIA can show short clips on the big screen
- Her own film is a major story moment
- Screen content system supports cutscenes

### Voice
- No voice acting
- Sound effects: Projector whir, film clatter, lens click
- Different sounds for different eras of quotes
