# Narrative Spec

## The Telos

The story exists to transform play into meaning. Every token feels more valuable when it's pushing back the dark. Every NPC feels more alive when you know what they've lost. The narrative elevates minigames from distractions into contributions.

## Story Delivery

### Environmental Storytelling
- Faded posters hint at past events
- NPC dialogue changes as restoration progresses
- Hidden collectibles reveal lore fragments
- Visual decay tells its own story

### Quest Chains
Each character has a personal quest line (5-7 quests) that:
- Reveals their backstory
- Contributes to zone restoration
- Unlocks exclusive rewards
- Connects to the main narrative

### Main Story Beats
Triggered by restoration milestones, delivered through cutscenes and special events.

## Act Structure

### PROLOGUE: The Flicker

**Trigger**: First login

**Scene**:
A screen flickers. Static clears. You're standing in a vast, dim lobby. Dust motes float in shafts of light from a dying chandelier. The floor is checkered marble, cracked in places. Ahead, a massive fountain stands dry, its token slot tarnished.

A keyboard floats toward you, keys clacking.

> QWERTY: "H-HELLO? Is this thing on? CAPS LOCK—no, wait—"
> QWERTY: "A PLAYER! An actual PLAYER! Do you know how long it's been?"
> QWERTY: "Welcome to Neocadia. Or... what's left of it."

**Telos Established**: You're special because you found your way here. The arcade needs you.

**Ludos Introduced**: Tokens generate from play. Tokens restore things. The fountain is the first goal.

---

### ACT 1: AWAKENING

**Chapters**: 1-3
**Zones Available**: Lobby, Pixel Beach, Neon Alley
**Restoration Range**: 0-25%

#### Chapter 1: First Light

**Goal**: Earn 500 tokens, restore fountain

**Beats**:
- QWERTY teaches basic navigation
- Discover Pixel Beach (unlocked by default)
- Meet PIXEL, play first minigame
- Earn enough tokens to restore the Token Fountain
- Fountain restoration cutscene: warm light spreads through Lobby

**PIXEL Introduction**:
> PIXEL: "...oh. a visitor. 'scuse the mess. tide's been weird."
> PIXEL: "you play games, yeah? i got games. nothing fancy."
> PIXEL: "just... nice to see someone. y'know?"

#### Chapter 2: The Beat Returns

**Goal**: Unlock Neon Alley (500 tokens)

**Beats**:
- QWERTY mentions the dark corridor
- Spend tokens to power the Neon Alley door
- Lights flicker on one by one down the hallway
- Meet NEON (initially glitchy, stabilizes as you approach)
- First competitive minigame

**NEON Introduction**:
> NEON: "PLAYER—ONE—READY—"
> NEON: *stabilizes* "Sorry! Still booting up. It's been a WHILE."
> NEON: "You want HIGH SCORES? I got high scores. The highest!"
> NEON: "Well. I USED to. Now they're just... memories in the walls."

#### Chapter 3: Whispers of Before

**Goal**: Reach 25% total restoration

**Beats**:
- NPCs begin mentioning "the old days"
- Find first lore collectible (a faded photo of Axiom Interactive team)
- QWERTY reveals they were "the first program ever written for this place"
- Unlock either Clockwork Quarter OR Sugar Rush Boulevard
- First hint of the Static: a dark patch that resists restoration

**Mystery Hook**:
> QWERTY: "That shadow? Don't—don't look at it too long."
> QWERTY: "It's always been there. At the edges."
> QWERTY: "It's what happens when no one remembers."

---

### ACT 2: REMEMBERING

**Chapters**: 4-6
**Zones Available**: All except Glitch Garden (zones unlock progressively as restoration increases—see Economy spec for prerequisites)
**Restoration Range**: 25-60%

> **Note**: Players unlock zones progressively during Act 2. Sugar Rush (25%), Clockwork Quarter (35%), and Starlight Cinema (45%) become available as overall restoration increases. The narrative accommodates players having different zones available at different times.

#### Chapter 4: The Clockwork Stutter

**Goal**: Restore Clockwork Quarter to Awakening stage

**Beats**:
- PROFESSOR COG is frozen mid-motion when you arrive
- Playing games in the zone gradually "winds" him back up
- He speaks of "the Great Engine" that once powered everything
- Reveals that Neocadia didn't always need player-generated power
- Quest: Find the missing gears scattered across other zones

**COG's Revelation**:
> COG: "The Engine, you see, was meant to make us self-sufficient."
> COG: "But something went wrong. Someone tried to... turn it off."
> COG: "Now we run on borrowed time. Your time."
> COG: "I don't mean to seem ungrateful. But it's not sustainable, is it?"

#### Chapter 5: Sweet Melancholy

**Goal**: Restore Sugar Rush Boulevard to Awakening stage

**Beats**:
- SUCRE is melting when you first meet them
- They're desperate for company, almost manic in their cheerfulness
- Playing games solidifies their form
- They mention "The Original Recipe" that gave them consciousness
- Quest: Gather ingredients from other zones

**SUCRE's Confession**:
> SUCRE: "Do you know what happens to candy no one eats?"
> SUCRE: "We get... sticky. We blend together. We forget what flavor we were."
> SUCRE: "Please stay. Play the games. I promise they're sweet!"
> SUCRE: "I don't want to forget my flavor."

#### Chapter 6: Silver Screen Dreams

**Goal**: Restore Starlight Cinema to Awakening stage

**Beats**:
- CELIA's projector light is dim, barely visible
- The films she shows are fragmentary, missing reels
- She speaks entirely in movie quotes, desperately trying to communicate
- Quest: Find the scattered Film Reels across Neocadia
- As reels are found, she begins speaking in her own voice

**CELIA's Awakening**:
> CELIA: *in fragments* "Here's looking... at the end... of a beautiful..."
> CELIA: *clearer* "Wait. Those aren't my words."
> CELIA: *fully present* "Oh. Oh, darling. I've been quoting for so long."
> CELIA: "I forgot what my own voice sounds like."

---

### ACT 3: THE GARDEN

**Chapters**: 7-8
**Zones Available**: All
**Restoration Range**: 60-85%

#### Chapter 7: Through the Glitch

**Goal**: Unlock and enter the Glitch Garden (2000 tokens)

**Beats**:
- All other NPCs warn against the garden
- But they also admit they've seen someone there
- The unlock is expensive—it feels like a threshold
- The Garden is beautiful: corrupted roses, inverted waterfalls, broken symmetries
- Meet VERA

**First Encounter**:
> VERA: "You're... not supposed to... be here."
> VERA: "No one comes... to the garden... anymore."
> VERA: "I was... someone. Once. I think."
> VERA: "Do you... remember... me?"

#### Chapter 8: Fragments

**Goal**: Complete VERA's quest line to 50%

**Beats**:
- VERA's memories are scattered as glitch samples throughout Neocadia
- Collecting them restores fragments of her speech, her form
- Each fragment reveals a piece of the truth:
  - She was the creator
  - She tried to shut Neocadia down
  - She became trapped instead
- Other NPCs react to these revelations

**The Others React**:
> QWERTY: "She... made me? I have a maker?"
> NEON: "She tried to END US? And we're supposed to HELP her?"
> PIXEL: "...she must've had her reasons. she was probably tired."
> COG: "If she's the engineer... she can fix the Engine."
> SUCRE: "She gave me my recipe! My flavor! I REMEMBER now!"
> CELIA: "Every great story needs its author. Even if the ending was rewritten."

---

### ACT 4: THE CHOICE

**Chapters**: 9-10
**Zones Available**: All
**Restoration Range**: 85-100%

#### Chapter 9: The Engine Room

**Goal**: Reach 100% restoration, unlock the Engine Room

**Beats**:
- Full restoration reveals a hidden door in the Lobby
- The Engine Room contains the Great Engine—dormant but intact
- VERA, now mostly coherent, explains:
  - The Engine could make Neocadia self-sustaining
  - But activating it requires a choice
- All NPCs gather

**VERA's Explanation**:
> VERA: "The Engine runs on purpose. On intention."
> VERA: "When I tried to shut this place down, I gave it the wrong purpose."
> VERA: "Now it's frozen, waiting for a new command."
> VERA: "Someone has to tell it what Neocadia is FOR."

#### Chapter 10: The Command

**Goal**: Make the final choice

Three options, each with consequences:

---

### ENDING A: THE ETERNAL ARCADE

**Choice**: Program the Engine to sustain Neocadia forever, independent of players

**Consequences**:
- Neocadia becomes a perpetual preserve
- NPCs become truly immortal
- VERA is freed—her consciousness moves on
- Players can still visit, but they're no longer necessary
- The Static is locked out permanently

**Final Scene**:
> VERA: "You've made them safe. Forever."
> VERA: "I can... rest now. Thank you."
> VERA: "Keep playing. Not because you have to."
> VERA: "Because it's fun."

*Neocadia glows eternal. The player receives the "ETERNAL PLAYER" title. Postgame: All zones at maximum brightness, special cosmetics, but VERA is gone.*

---

### ENDING B: THE GENTLE FADE

**Choice**: Allow the Static to slowly reclaim Neocadia, making what remains precious

**Consequences**:
- Neocadia will eventually end
- But the ending will be natural, peaceful
- NPCs accept their mortality, live more fully
- VERA stays, wanting to see it through
- The Static becomes beautiful—sunset instead of death

**Final Scene**:
> VERA: "Nothing lasts forever. That's what makes it matter."
> VERA: "We'll have time. Years, maybe. Decades if people keep coming."
> VERA: "And when the last light goes out..."
> VERA: "We'll have been real. For a while."

*Neocadia gains a twilight aesthetic. The player receives the "LAST LIGHT" title. Postgame: Bittersweet tone, some zones slowly dim over real-world months, extremely rare seasonal resurgences.*

---

### ENDING C: THE MERGE

**Choice**: Let VERA fully integrate with Neocadia, becoming its living heart

**Consequences**:
- VERA becomes the Engine
- Neocadia becomes truly alive—responsive, evolving
- NPCs gain new awareness, agency
- The Static is absorbed, transformed into something new
- The arcade grows beyond its original boundaries

**Final Scene**:
> VERA: "I tried to end this place because I thought it was time."
> VERA: "But maybe... I was meant to become it."
> VERA: "Thank you for bringing me home."
> VERA: "Welcome to Neocadia. I'll be here. Always."

*Neocadia transforms—new zones appear over time, NPCs develop new storylines, the world actively responds to player behavior. The player receives the "FIRST FRIEND" title. Postgame: Most dynamic experience, new content appears based on collective player activity.*

## Quest Summaries

### QWERTY: "SYSTEM RESTORE"
1. Find the original boot code (hidden in Lobby)
2. Debug corrupted memories (minigame)
3. Recover keyboard keys lost in other zones
4. Learn QWERTY's first memory
5. Final: Decide whether QWERTY should remember their creation

### NEON: "HIGH SCORE HISTORY"
1. Recover faded high scores from Neon Alley walls
2. Investigate the legendary "AAA" who topped every leaderboard
3. Find AAA's old cabinet (hidden)
4. Discover AAA was the first player ever
5. Final: Enter your name in the Hall of Legends or restore AAA's legacy

### PIXEL: "THE GREAT SANDCASTLE"
1. Gather pixel sand from across Neocadia
2. Restore the sandcastle's foundation
3. Defend against tide of static
4. Find the shell that was the castle's heart
5. Final: Complete the castle and discover it was PIXEL's original home

### VERA: "DEFRAGMENTATION"
1. Collect glitch fragments from all zones
2. Reassemble VERA's memories
3. Confront the moment of shutdown
4. Understand why she chose to end it
5. Final: Help her choose whether to stay or go

### CELIA: "THE LAST PICTURE SHOW"
1. Find scattered film reels (7 total)
2. Each reel is a different genre, needs different approach
3. Assemble her unfinished masterpiece
4. Watch it together
5. Final: Decide the ending of her film—and her story

### PROFESSOR COG: "THE GREAT ENGINE"
1. Recover blueprints from Clockwork Quarter archives
2. Gather gears from all zones
3. Solve the Engine's mechanical puzzles
4. Understand why it was sabotaged
5. Final: Help COG accept that perfection isn't the point

### SUCRE: "THE ORIGINAL RECIPE"
1. Find recipe fragments from Sugar Rush history
2. Gather ingredients from every zone
3. Attempt to recreate the formula
4. Each failed attempt creates a new Sucre variant
5. Final: Accept that Sucre is more than their recipe

## NPC Travel Rules

NPCs are primarily zone-bound but can travel for story events.

### Default Positions
| NPC | Home Zone | Default Location |
|-----|-----------|------------------|
| QWERTY | Lobby | Registry Desk |
| NEON | Neon Alley | Stage |
| PIXEL | Pixel Beach | Shack |
| CELIA | Starlight Cinema | Projection Room |
| COG | Clockwork Quarter | Workshop |
| SUCRE | Sugar Rush | Candy Stand |
| VERA | Glitch Garden | VERA's Willow |

### Travel Permissions
- **QWERTY**: Can follow player to any zone for tutorial moments and story beats
- **NEON, PIXEL, CELIA, COG, SUCRE**: Can travel to Lobby for gatherings (Act 3+, story triggers)
- **VERA**: Cannot leave Glitch Garden until Act 4 (her fragmented state prevents travel)

### When NPCs Gather (Lobby)
Story triggers that bring NPCs together:
- Act 3 revelation scene (all NPCs learn about VERA)
- Act 4 final choice scene (all NPCs present in Engine Room/Lobby)
- Individual NPCs may appear in Lobby for quest handoffs

### Implementation
- NPCs teleport between zones (no walking animation needed)
- Travel happens during scene transitions
- Player cannot witness NPCs traveling
- After story scenes, NPCs return to home zones

## Tone Guidelines

### When Players Arrive
- Wonder and invitation
- Mystery and discovery
- "This place needs you" without guilt

### During Restoration
- Hope and momentum
- Small victories matter
- NPCs react and grow

### In the Glitch Garden
- Bittersweetness
- Beauty in decay
- Approaching the truth

### At the Choice
- Weight without judgment
- All endings are valid
- The player's values decide

### Postgame
- Cozy persistence
- Always more to discover
- Home to return to
