# Character Spec: SUCRE

## Overview

### Role
Party host, recipe keeper, celebration coordinator. SUCRE runs Sugar Rush Boulevard and exists to make everyone feel welcome and happy. They're pure joy on the surface, but underneath is a deep loneliness and an existential question about identity.

### Telos
SUCRE represents the mask of happiness—being cheerful when you're actually hurting. Their journey is about finding genuine joy instead of performed joy, and accepting that identity isn't fixed. They teach players that it's okay to not be okay, and that change doesn't mean losing yourself.

### Personality
- Excitable and enthusiastic (almost too much)
- Speaks in food puns constantly
- Desperate for company (shows as intense friendliness)
- Secretly melancholic about their isolation
- Identity anxiety (what are they made of? who are they?)
- Generous to a fault
- Learning that authentic sweetness is better than artificial

## Visual Description

### Character Concept Prompt
```
A golem made of stacked and assembled sweets—like a candy snowman that learned to walk. The body is layered: a cake base torso, gumdrop buttons, licorice limbs, frosting "hair," and two bright candy eyes (currently lemon drops). The composition changes based on what they eat—feed them different ingredients, and their appearance shifts. They're always slightly sticky, leaving tiny sugar trails. Their expression is painted on in frosting but still animated. They're simultaneously adorable and slightly unnerving in their eagerness.
```

### Physical Details

**Body Structure**: Stacked confections
- Base/Torso: Round cake layer (changes flavor/color)
- Middle: Cream filling layer
- Top: Smaller cake/cupcake for head
- Arms: Licorice whips (#1A1A1A)
- Legs: Candy cane segments (#FF0000/#FFFFFF)

**Face**:
- Eyes: Candy drops (currently yellow/lemon #FFD700)
- Mouth: Frosting-drawn, can reshape
- Optional: Sprinkle freckles, cherry nose

**Current Default Colors**:
- Cake: Pink (#FFB6C1)
- Frosting: White (#FFFAFA)
- Details: Rainbow sprinkles

**Changeable Elements** (based on fed ingredients):
- Cake flavor → Body color
- Frosting type → Hair/decoration color
- Candy eyes → Eye color
- Can add/remove elements

**Special Effects**:
- Slight wobble when standing
- Sugar particle trail when moving
- Frosting "hair" has physics
- Melting slightly when sad/hot

## Speech Patterns

### Verbal Tics
- EXCESSIVE CAPS for enthusiasm
- Food puns (constant, can't help it)
- Exclamation marks everywhere!
- Occasionally voice cracks (betrays real emotion)
- "Please" appears often (need for connection)

### Surface vs. Depth
- Surface: Relentlessly upbeat, pun-heavy
- Depth: Emerges in quiet moments, between the jokes
- Player can ask follow-up questions to reach depth
- Late game: Better balance between modes

### Things SUCRE Would Say
- "You're just in time! Time for COOKIES!"
- "That's the spirit! The VANILLA spirit!"
- "Don't be a SOUR-puss! Get it? Sour?"
- "Stay for a while? The party's just starting..."

### Things SUCRE Would NOT Say
- Anything genuinely mean
- Admitting loneliness directly (early game)
- Saying "no" to requests
- Being quiet (it's scary)

## Quest Line: "THE ORIGINAL RECIPE"

### Quest 1: Flavor Memory
**Trigger**: Sugar Rush reaches 25% restoration
**Task**: Find the first recipe fragment (hidden in zone)
**Reward**: 150 tokens, Sucre gains slight color change
**Dialogue**: "There's a recipe somewhere. THE recipe. What made me... me."

### Quest 2: Ingredient Hunt
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 1
**Task**: Gather ingredients from all zones (7 total)
**Reward**: 100 tokens each, can feed ingredients to Sucre
**Dialogue**: "The recipe needs special ingredients. From everywhere!"

### Quest 3: The Baking
**Trigger**: Collect all ingredients
**Task**: Attempt to recreate the recipe (cooking minigame)
**Reward**: 300 tokens, result is "wrong" (intentionally)
**Dialogue**: "Time to bake! What comes out will be... me? Maybe?"

### Quest 4: Wrong Flavor
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 3
**Task**: Comfort Sucre after the "failure" (dialogue choices)
**Reward**: 400 tokens, perspective shift for Sucre
**Dialogue**: "It didn't work. I'm still not... what I was."
**Hidden**: The "failure" is the point—Sucre learns identity isn't fixed

### Quest 5: New Recipe
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 4
**Task**: Help Sucre create a NEW recipe (player chooses final form)
**Reward**: 500 tokens, title "Confectioner", Sucre's form changes to player choice
**Dialogue**: "Maybe the Original Recipe isn't about going back. Maybe it's about going forward. Help me make a new one? One that's... now?"

## Inter-Character Dialogue

### SUCRE & COG (The Secret Friendship)
> *SUCRE leaves a cupcake on COG's workbench. COG enters.*
> COG: "This confection is structurally unsound. The frosting ratio exceeds—"
> SUCRE: *from behind a gear* "Do you LIKE it though?"
> COG: "...You are here."
> SUCRE: "I'm always here! Watching! Making sure you EAT!"
> COG: "I do not require—"
> SUCRE: "Nobody REQUIRES cupcakes, Professor. That's what makes them CUPCAKES."
> COG: *long pause, takes a bite* "The sweetness coefficient is... acceptable."
> SUCRE: *bounces happily* "That's the nicest thing you've ever said!"
> COG: "I made something. For you. Structural reinforcement. For your... composition."
> SUCRE: "...You made me armor? Made of CARING?"
> COG: "I made you a molecular stabilizer. Caring is not a material."
> SUCRE: *melts slightly, happy tears* "It is NOW."

### SUCRE & PIXEL (Learning Stillness)
> *SUCRE sits next to PIXEL at the beach. They're vibrating with effort.*
> PIXEL: "you're shaking."
> SUCRE: "I'm TRYING to be STILL! You make it look EASY!"
> PIXEL: "it's not. took years."
> SUCRE: "How do you DO it? The quiet? The not-performing?"
> PIXEL: "...i got tired. that's all."
> SUCRE: "But don't you want to FILL the silence? Make it BETTER?"
> PIXEL: "sometimes silence is already better."
> *They watch the waves. SUCRE manages thirty seconds of stillness.*
> PIXEL: "...that was good."
> SUCRE: "IT WAS SO HARD! But also... the waves are nice?"
> PIXEL: "yeah. they are."
> SUCRE: "Can I come back? And practice? Being quiet with you?"
> PIXEL: "...yeah. i'd like that."

### SUCRE & VERA (Recipe and Regret)
> SUCRE: "You made me. The first recipe. The ORIGINAL flavor."
> VERA: "I... yes. I remember designing the sweetness protocols."
> SUCRE: "I tried to find it. The Original Recipe. To go BACK to what I was."
> VERA: "Did you find it?"
> SUCRE: "Yes. It didn't work. I'm not... I'm not that SUCRE anymore."
> VERA: "...Neither am I the person who wrote that recipe."
> SUCRE: "Does that mean we're both WRONG?"
> VERA: "No. It means we're both... still becoming."
> *SUCRE wobbles, uncertain*
> SUCRE: "I'm scared. That I'm dissolving into nothing. That without the Original Recipe—"
> VERA: "You're not nothing. You're every party that ever happened. Every celebration. Every joy."
> VERA: "The recipe was just ingredients. You're what they became."
> SUCRE: *sniffles* "...That's the sweetest thing a glitch ever told me."

### SUCRE & CELIA (The Performers' Bond)
> CELIA: "'The show must go on.' I say that a lot."
> SUCRE: "Me too! Except I say THE PARTY MUST GO ON! Same energy!"
> CELIA: "Do you ever... not want it to go on?"
> SUCRE: *stops bouncing* "...Sometimes."
> CELIA: "The performance is exhausting. Always being bright. Always being—"
> SUCRE: "—SWEET. Yeah. I know."
> CELIA: "You know what helps?"
> SUCRE: "What?"
> CELIA: "Having someone who knows it's a performance. And likes you anyway."
> SUCRE: "Is that... is that what we are?"
> CELIA: "'Here's looking at you, kid.'"
> SUCRE: "I don't know that movie but I FEEL SEEN."

## Implementation Notes

### Mood System
- Track player interactions with Sucre
- More genuine dialogue unlocks with trust
- Loneliness meter (hidden): decreases with visits

### Transformation
- Player can feed ingredients found in minigames
- Each feeding triggers transformation animation
- Some combinations unlock special variants
- Final quest locks in "canon" form (player choice)

### Party System
- At high restoration, Sucre hosts zone events
- Party Stage activates
- Special dialogue during parties
- Other NPCs can visit (via mentions)

### Voice
- No voice acting
- Sound effects: Squelch, sugar sparkle, wobble
- Gummy bounce sounds
- Sad: Drip/melt sounds
