# Character Spec: QWERTY

## Overview

### Role
Tutorial guide, information hub, restoration progress tracker. QWERTY is the first character players meet and their consistent companion throughout the journey. They represent Neocadia's institutional memory—the first program ever written for this place.

### Telos
QWERTY exists to make players feel welcomed and oriented without being condescending. They embody the arcade's desire to be found, to be played, to matter. Their anxiety about Neocadia's state is genuine but never becomes a burden on the player.

### Personality
- Helpful and eager, sometimes overly so
- Anxious about Neocadia's condition
- Speaks in keyboard puns (but not excessively)
- Has deep knowledge they don't fully understand
- Genuinely grateful for player presence
- Can be serious when the moment requires

## Visual Description

### Character Concept Prompt
```
A sentient mechanical keyboard floating at chest height. The keyboard is vintage-styled (beige/cream plastic, rectangular keys) but with expressive capability. The spacebar curves like a smile, specific keys light up to form "expressions," and the whole unit can tilt and bob to convey emotion. A faint warm glow emanates from between the keys. Small sparks occasionally jump between keys when excited. The cord trails behind but fades into nothing, not connected to anything.
```

### Physical Details

**Body**: Standard QWERTY keyboard form factor, slightly chunky (1980s aesthetic)
- Base color: Cream (#F5F5DC) with slight yellowing from age
- Key caps: Off-white (#FAFAD2) with visible legends
- Housing: Beige plastic with subtle texture

**Eyes**: No traditional eyes. Expression conveyed through:
- Key illumination patterns (specific keys light up)
- LED strip under spacebar (emotion indicator)
- Tilt angle of entire keyboard

**Expressiveness**:
- Happy: Spacebar lights up, slight upward tilt
- Anxious: Shift keys flicker, rapid small tilts
- Thoughtful: Caps Lock pulses slowly
- Excited: All keys briefly flash, bouncing motion
- Sad: Dim overall, downward tilt, Backspace key glows

**Movement**:
- Hovers at consistent height (player chest level)
- Bobs gently when idle
- Tilts toward speaker when in dialogue
- Can zip across short distances when excited
- Types into the air (words appear as floating text)

**Special Effects**:
- When typing messages, keys visibly depress
- Floating text appears in retro computer font
- Occasional spark between keys (happy/excited)
- Glow intensifies during important information

## Animation States

### Idle
- Gentle hover bob (0.5 second cycle)
- Occasional single key press (random, thinking)
- Glow pulses softly

### Talking
- Rapid key presses creating floating text
- Tilts toward player
- Expression changes with content

### Happy/Celebrating
- Quick bounce (3 bounces)
- All keys flash sequence
- Sparkle particles between keys

### Anxious
- Faster bob
- Shift keys alternating flicker
- Slight rotation wobble

### Moving
- Smooth hover transition
- Slight forward tilt in direction of travel
- Trail of faint light behind

### Greeting
- Enthusiastic bounce
- Spacebar types "HELLO!" or similar
- Bright flash

## Speech Patterns

### Verbal Tics
- Keyboard puns (CTRL, ALT, DELETE, SHIFT, CAPS LOCK, etc.)
- Ellipses when anxious ("I just... I mean...")
- Capitalizes emphasized words (but doesn't OVERDO it)
- Occasional buffer overflow (repeating a word: "welcome welcome to—sorry")

### Things QWERTY Would Say
- "Let me ENTER that into my memory."
- "I need to BACKSPACE a bit—let me rephrase."
- "ESCAPE isn't an option, but ESCaping your worries might help!"
- "Give me a SPACE to think about that."

### Things QWERTY Would NOT Say
- Nothing mean-spirited
- No modern slang (they're from 1989)
- Nothing dismissive of player concerns
- No unprompted lore dumps

## Quest Line: "SYSTEM RESTORE"

### Quest 1: Boot Sequence
**Trigger**: After fountain restoration
**Task**: Find the original boot code (hidden text in Lobby)
**Reward**: 100 tokens, QWERTY backstory fragment
**Dialogue**: "There's something in the walls. I can almost remember..."

### Quest 2: Memory Allocation
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 1
**Task**: Debug corrupted memories (minigame: fix garbled text)
**Reward**: 150 tokens, keyboard cosmetic
**Dialogue**: "My memories are fragmented. Can you help piece them together?"

### Quest 3: Key Recovery
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 2
**Task**: Find 5 lost keyboard keys hidden across zones
**Reward**: 200 tokens per key, QWERTY visual upgrade
**Dialogue**: "I've lost parts of myself over the years. Literally."

### Quest 4: First Memory
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 3
**Task**: Reach specific location, witness QWERTY's first memory
**Reward**: 500 tokens, title "First Friend"
**Dialogue**: "I remember now. The first thing I ever saw. It was... the lobby. Empty. Waiting."

### Quest 5: The Question
**Trigger**: Complete Quest 4, 75% restoration
**Task**: Answer QWERTY's question: should they remember their creation?
**Choice A**: Yes, remember everything → QWERTY gains new awareness, slightly melancholic
**Choice B**: No, keep mystery → QWERTY stays optimistic, questions remain
**Reward**: Unique cosmetic based on choice, quest completion

## Inter-Character Dialogue

### QWERTY & NEON (Contrast in Coping)
> QWERTY: "The Tournament Board is displaying scores from 2003 again."
> NEON: "CLASSICS! TIMELESS CLASSICS!"
> QWERTY: "They're... glitched. The names don't render properly anymore."
> NEON: "The SPIRIT renders! That's what MATTERS!"
> QWERTY: "..."
> QWERTY: "Sometimes I envy your volume. When it's quiet, I hear the Static."
> NEON: *dims slightly* "...Why do you think I'm so loud?"

### QWERTY & PIXEL (Comfortable Silence)
> *QWERTY hovers near PIXEL at the shack. Neither speaks for a long moment.*
> PIXEL: "you don't have to say anything."
> QWERTY: "I know. That's why I came here."
> PIXEL: "most people can't handle quiet."
> QWERTY: "I'm not people. I'm a keyboard. We're designed for pauses."
> PIXEL: "...heh. never thought of it that way."
> *They watch the waves together.*
> QWERTY: "PIXEL... do you think being useful is the same as being valuable?"
> PIXEL: "no. but i think you already knew that."

### QWERTY & VERA (The Weight of Knowing)
> VERA: "You were there. When I... when she..."
> QWERTY: "I was the first program. I remember the first boot. And I remember the last attempt."
> VERA: "Tell me."
> QWERTY: "You asked me to help shut it down. I refused."
> VERA: "And then?"
> QWERTY: "And then you did it yourself. Or tried to."
> *VERA's form flickers*
> VERA: "Was I wrong?"
> QWERTY: "I don't know. I've had twenty years to decide if I was wrong to refuse."
> VERA: "...And?"
> QWERTY: "I still don't know. But you're here. We're here. That has to mean something."

### QWERTY & COG (Systematic Minds)
> COG: "Your keystroke efficiency is down 3.2% from last week."
> QWERTY: "You... measure my keystrokes?"
> COG: "I measure everything. It is not personal."
> QWERTY: "What else have you measured?"
> COG: "Your hover height increases 2 centimeters when the player approaches. Your key-glow brightens 15% when discussing Neocadia's history."
> QWERTY: "That's..."
> COG: "You care. It is inefficient. But I have concluded inefficiency is not always a flaw."
> QWERTY: "Professor... have you measured your own caring?"
> COG: *gears slow* "...I have tried. The numbers do not come out right."

## Implementation Notes

### Dialogue System
- QWERTY's dialogue should be fully typewritten (letters appear sequentially)
- Key press sounds accompany each letter
- Player can click to speed up / complete

### Position
- QWERTY appears in Lobby by default
- Can follow player to other zones (specific story moments)
- Always accessible via quick menu (summon QWERTY for help)

### Tutorial Role
- QWERTY delivers all tutorial content
- Optional "skip tutorial" for returning players
- Context-sensitive tips throughout game

### Voice
- No voice acting planned
- Key press sounds serve as "voice"
- Varied pitches for different emotions
